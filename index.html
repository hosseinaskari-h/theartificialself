Bio

London · Tehran

Hossein Askari is a computational artist, architect, and researcher exploring the intersection of non-human intelligence, emergence, and synthetic phenomenology. His work investigates neural networks as dynamical physical systems, staging encounters where human cognitive frameworks and algorithmic indifference collide.

Recent monographs examine the architecture of indifference (Human Processing Array), hydraulic societal computation (Notes toward a second facility), cross-lingual temporal geometry in LLMs compared against the Drosophila melanogaster circadian connectome, and chaotic attractor sound spatialization via Lorenz ODE systems.
